Support

We will actually write back.

Walden Hollow is made by a very small team, which means there is no ticket queue and no phone tree, but it also means a real person reads every message and usually answers the same day.

Email: hello@waldenhollow.app
Typical reply: same day, and always within two business days.

Before you write, this helps a lot

You do not need to do any of this, but if you can, it saves a round trip:

  • Which club you are in, and the email address you sign in with
  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead
  • Whether it happens every time, or happened once
  • Your phone (iPhone or Android) and, if you know it, the app version, which is at the bottom of Settings
  • A screenshot, if there was something on the screen worth seeing

Things we get asked most

I cannot sign in.

Check the email address first. It must be the one your club's administrator added, and it is easy to have two. If it is right and it still will not let you in, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. If that does not arrive within a few minutes, check the spam folder, then write to us and we will sort it out directly.

The app says my account is not set up yet.

That means your sign-in worked but your club administrator has not added you to the club register. Ask them to add you in Settings → Manage Members. It takes them about ten seconds. If they are stuck, send us their name and we will help them.

My photo did not attach to a catch.

Usually this is the photo permission. On iPhone: Settings → Walden Hollow → Photos and Camera, and make sure both are allowed. If the permission is on and it still fails, tell us roughly when you took it and we will look. We would want to know either way.

The nearest hole is wrong, or it says it cannot find me.

The app needs location permission while it is open. On iPhone: Settings → Walden Hollow → Location → While Using the App. Note that canyons and heavy tree cover genuinely degrade GPS. If it is close but not exact, you can always pick the hole from the list instead.

A hole is missing, or has the wrong name.

Hole names and positions are your club's, and your club's administrator can add, rename, or move them in Settings → Manage Holes. If a whole stretch is wrong, write to us. We set these up originally and we will fix them properly.

The river conditions look wrong or are missing.

Flow and temperature come from public U.S. Geological Survey gauges, which occasionally go offline for maintenance or ice. When that happens the app says the reading is unavailable rather than showing an old number as if it were current. If it stays blank for more than a day, tell us and we will check which gauge your club is pointed at.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete my account. What happens to your catches is explained on the privacy page. If you cannot get into the app, email us and we will do it for you.

Is there an Android version?

It is built and not yet released. If you are on Android and want to be told when it is ready, email us and we will put you on the list.

For club administrators

If you run a club and need holes remapped, members added in bulk, a season report, or an export of everything the club has recorded, write to us directly. That is not self-service and it is not meant to be.

Reporting something security-related

If you think you have found a way to see data you should not be able to see, please tell us before telling anyone else: hello@waldenhollow.app. We take it seriously, we will respond quickly, and we will credit you if you would like us to.
